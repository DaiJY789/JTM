使用方法

  1. 选择“多模态事实核查”或“深度伪造检测”，点击“新对话”。
  2. 右侧准备输入：填写正文、上传文件，或粘贴链接并点击“导入”。多模态事实核查必须有文本，图片可选；深度伪造检测需上传一张图片。
  3. 检查正文和图片预览，移除不需要的配图。
  4. 点击“开始检测”，查看顶部结果和“核查结论与依据”。首次检测时，对话输入框可留空，也可填写希望重点核查的问题。
  5. 检测完成后，在对话框输入问题并发送，继续深入了解本次结果。
  1. Select “Multimodal fact-checking” or “Deepfake detection”, then click “New Chat”.
  2. Prepare your input on the right. Enter text, upload a file, or paste a link and click “Import”. Multimodal fact-checking requires text, with optional images. Deepfake detection requires one image.
  3. Review the text and image previews, and remove any images you do not need.
  4. Click “Detect” to view the top results and “Verdict and evidence”. For the initial check, leave the message field empty or enter a question to focus the analysis.
  5. After detection, enter a question in the conversation and click “Send” to explore the result further.
